Typed MCP surface / proposed

Production requires a hosted MCP server, authenticated identities, server-side authorization, durable queues, secrets, destination APIs and an external audit store. GitHub Pages hosts this interface only.
Production boundaries
Policy service: deny by default, enforce account scope, freshness, authority, approval expiry and argument binding.
Execution worker: persist idempotency keys, reconcile unknown outcomes by reading destination state before retrying.
Evidence service: immutable storage, trusted timestamps, access controls and signed exports.
Model service: versioned prompts, retrieval provenance and evaluated classifiers. No live model is called in this file.
Passing a browser simulation does not prove production parity. Reuse the policy specification and run contract tests against real adapters.

Rules / explicit assumptions
Explainable by design.
Score and trigger contract
15 signals / 100 points: telemetry 30, support 20, relationships 15, adoption 15, commercial 20. Onboarding: 10 / 20 / 15 / 40 / 15.
Thriving ≥85; Healthy ≥70; Watch ≥50; At Risk ≥30; Critical <30. Bands use unrounded scores.
Fresh coverage: High ≥85%, Medium ≥60%, Low ≥40%, Insufficient <40%. Missing values receive a provisional neutral value; they reduce coverage. Inapplicable values redistribute weight only inside their pillar.
P1 >72h and explicit non-renewal cap score below 50. AR ≥1.5× MRR caps below 70. Healthy / Thriving require ≥60% fresh coverage in every pillar and overall Medium confidence.
Immediate P1 paging is independent of CHI. Recovery is limited to +6 points per week; deterioration is immediate. A better band requires two qualifying runs at least 5 points above its threshold.
Signal weights, thresholds, neutral values and time windows are modeled assumptions requiring calibration. No revenue uplift is inferred.
